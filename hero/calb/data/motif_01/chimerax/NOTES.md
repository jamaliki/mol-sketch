# Viewing the CALB pNPA cycle in ChimeraX

    cd /Users/kiarash.jamali/Desktop/Code/mechazyme/exports/calb_pnpa_animation_v1/motif_01/chimerax
    open play.cxc       # loads 201 frames as model #1, styles it, draws the reacting pairs
    open record.cxc     # optional: encodes ~/Desktop/calb_pnpa_cycle.mp4

`play.cxc` was run headlessly against ChimeraX 1.10.1 and completes cleanly:
201 frames of 75 atoms load as a coordinate set, and `coordset #1 1,201` plays.
Rendering and encoding were not verified here — this shell has no OpenGL. Run
those in the GUI.

## Do not use `morph`

ChimeraX's `morph` mangles this structure. Measured on morph's own output, it
compresses a nitro N–O bond from 1.24 Å to **0.64 Å**, and an Asp carboxylate
C–O to 0.94 Å. `method linear`, `cartesian false` and `rate sinusoidal` all give
byte-identical results, so no option fixes it.

It is not the data: straight linear interpolation of that same N–O pair gives
**1.23 Å** at the midpoint. `morph` segments a structure on residue connectivity
along a polymer chain, and this model has no polymer — six disconnected HETATM
residues — so its rigid-core detection degenerates.

`build_trajectory.py` interpolates directly instead: each chemically rigid
fragment is carried by its own Kabsch fit with quaternion slerp, the small
internal residual is added linearly, and hydrogens are rebuilt in their parent's
local frame (direction slerped, length interpolated) so every X–H bond keeps its
length. Result over all 201 frames: **no bond outside 0.93×–1.09×** its length,
and keyframes reproduce the exported states to 0.0008 Å.

## What is in the movie

Four residues, 75 atoms: the substrate/products, Ser105, His224 and Gln106.

**Asp187 and Thr40 were dropped.** Both are inert in this motif — Asp187 sits
15 Å from His224 with no triad hydrogen bond, and Thr40 is 4.8–8.4 Å from the
oxyanion, so it is not the oxyanion hole. They were also the main source of the
wobble: the search re-posed them independently in every state (heavy-atom
internal RMSD up to 0.62 Å between consecutive states), so they are different
rotamers, not a motion, and no interpolation can make a rotamer jump look
physical.

**Gln106 was kept** even though the DSL also declares it a spectator: it is the
only oxyanion stabilizer, and the donor the charge gate actually accepted in both
tetrahedral intermediates. It is carried as a rigid body (shape frozen at
state_00, bonds exact) because it too changes internal shape between states.
To drop it as well:

    .venv/bin/python exports/calb_pnpa_animation_v1/build_trajectory.py motif_01 40 ORIENT,THR40,GLN106

Only Ser105 and His224 are inside mechazyme's gated protein set (19 atoms), and
they are the two that are genuinely rigid across the cycle — 0.03–0.22 Å internal
RMSD per step. Everything else was free to move.

## Keyframes

State *k* sits at frame **40k + 1**: 1, 41, 81, 121, 161, 201.

| 1 | 41 | first tetrahedral intermediate |

| 3 | 121 | second tetrahedral intermediate |
| 4 | 161 | acetic acid + Ser alkoxide |
| 5 | 201 | resting state restored |

Frames between keyframes are interpolation, not mechazyme output — a smooth path
between two computed endpoints, not a reaction coordinate and not a transition
state. Worth a caption line.

## Two display choices, neither a mechazyme result

1. **The water is parked 2.5 Å out** along the site-exit vector in states 0–1.
   Its state_02 pose collides in the earlier states (0.95 Å from the Ser proton
   in state_01), and the mechanism does not model where it waits.
2. **Every reacting bond is absent from the topology** and drawn as a dashed
   distance monitor. One topology cannot be correct in every frame. A side
   effect worth keeping: the two transferring protons end up with no bonds at
   all, so as red spheres they visibly fly between Ser, His and the leaving
   group. Because those pairs are unconstrained during interpolation, the
   acyl-enzyme Ser OG···C1 dashed distance dips to 1.17 Å around frame 100
   against a real 1.35 Å — cosmetic, on a dashed line.

## Key serials (identical in every frame)

C1 carbonyl **2**, oxyanion O2 **3**, ester/phenol O4 **4**, water O **14**,
HW1 **15**, Ser105 OG **17**, Ser HG/H21 **18**, His224 NE2 **27**,
Gln106 NE2 **34**. Full table in `trajectory_serials.json`.

Serials shift if you change which residues are dropped — `build_trajectory.py`
regenerates `play.cxc` with the correct ones, so re-run it rather than editing
the script by hand.

For a figure rather than a movie, use the six `../state_0*.pdb` files: they carry
each state's true connectivity and formal charges, and nothing is interpolated.
